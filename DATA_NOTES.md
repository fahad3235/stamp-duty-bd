One OCR trap was caught during verification: secondary summaries of the 2022 Schedule list Acknowledgement (b) as "Three hundred Taka"; the gazette text itself reads "Fifty Taka". The app uses the gazette figure (&#x9f3;50).

## Sale-deed companion charges (not in Schedule I)

These are charged by the sub-registry alongside stamp duty, confirmed 2026-11-10 from two independent sources:

- **Registration fee 1%** and **stamp duty 1.5%** &#x2014; landregistrationbd.com, "Document Registration Cost 2025" (authored by a Sub-Registrar), and trw.co, "Property Registration Fees Bangladesh 2026".
- **Local government tax 2%** in city corporation / cantonment areas, **3%** elsewhere &#x2014; same two sources, agreeing.
- **VAT on new flats: 2% up to 1,600 sq ft, 4.5% above** &#x2014; trw.co buying-property guide, 2026.
- **Seller's affidavit stamp paper &#x9f3;300** &#x2014; trw.co guide (matches Schedule I Art. 4, &#x9f3;300).
- **Source tax** (Income Tax Act 2023 ss. 125/126) is area/mouza-based under the Source Tax Rules 2026 &#x2014; no single rate exists, so the app takes it as a user-entered amount or percentage. Never hardcoded.
- **E-fee / page fees** are small and variable; the app does not include them and says so in the README.

## The October 2026 committee proposal (NOT applied)

On 18&#x2013;21 October 2026 the Financial Express and other outlets reported a government committee (led by former Land Secretary ASM Saleh Ahmed) recommending stamp duty be halved from 1.5% to 0.75%, registration fee cut to 0.75%, and urban LG tax cut to 1.5%. These are **recommendations to the Prime Minister's Office, not law**. The app uses the rates in force and carries a note in the UI and README saying so.

## Rounding

All percentage results round to the nearest whole Taka (Math.round). The &#x9f3;2 crore conveyance cap is applied after rounding; the difference is at most &#x9f3;1.

## What is NOT in the app

- Court fees (Court-fees Act 1870) &#x2014; a different statute.
- Stamp duty on share transfers by endorsement beyond the Schedule I entries above.
- Any live rate feed &#x2014; the app is 100% offline by design; rates are a snapshot verified 2026-11-10.
